Our Reading of the Brief
Where Apis stands today
Pan-India honey and dates business, more than 90% of sales in roughly equal parts. 60% general trade, 40% MT, e-
comm/Q-comm, CSD and CPC. Marketing is mostly sales promotion. Key rivals are Dabur, Patanjali, Saffola, Lion.
The problem statement
Apis has grown on offers, and that has cost it brand value.
● The BOGO tag: The brand is seen as a deal, which makes premiumisation hard.
● Lookalike shelf: Generic packaging blends Apis into the category.
Our read
In honey and dates, shoppers are asking, "Can I trust what's in this jar?" Most rivals answer with claims. Apis has proof: a 
100-year legacy, BRCGS Grade A and QR certificates. The opportunity is to move Apis from "the cheaper option" to "the 
sensible, certified choice for everyday family health".
The question we'll answer
How does Apis stop being known for its offers and start being known for something only Apis can say?

CAMPAIGN CONCEPT
Apis | Nov 2026 
Apis makes natural products that are good for you.
The brand can also champion and stand for wellness.
However, wellness is a crowded space in the food category, often 
associated with medicinal and health-related claims.
Our approach, therefore, is to suggest wellness rather than spell it 
out, letting the brand evoke a feeling of well-being.
Our products are more than just healthy.
They are tasty, enjoyable and part of life's little pleasures.
