## **GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY: COCA-COLA GLOBAL BEVERAGE DIVISION**
**Objective:** *Capture Gen Z & Young Professionals (18-35) with a hyper-digital, experiential GTM motion to drive +20% market share in 90 days.*

---

### **1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & POSITIONING STATEMENT**
**GTM Goals:**
- **Primary:** Acquire 5M new Gen Z/Young Professional consumers in 90 days via digital-first engagement and retail activation.
- **Secondary:** Increase brand affinity by 30% through experiential marketing and influencer co-creation.
- **Tertiary:** Secure 10K+ retail partnerships for exclusive product placements.

**Core Value Proposition:**
*"Coca-Cola: The Original Social Fuel. Refreshing moments that connect, energize, and define your story—anytime, anywhere."*

**Positioning Statement:**
*"For Gen Z and Young Professionals who crave authenticity and shared experiences, Coca-Cola is the iconic beverage that bridges digital culture and real-world connection—delivering refreshment with a side of rebellion, nostalgia, and community."*

---

### **2. TARGET AUDIENCE & ICP PROFILES**

#### **Primary ICP: Gen Z & Young Professionals (18-35)**
| **Attribute**       | **Details**                                                                 |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pain Points**     | - Lack of authentic brand connections.                                     |
|                     | - Overwhelmed by digital noise; seeks "real" experiences.                  |
|                     | - Price-sensitive but willing to pay for exclusivity.                      |
| **Buying Triggers** | - Social validation (influencer endorsements, UGC).                        |
|                     | - FOMO-driven (limited-edition drops, event tie-ins).                      |
|                     | - Convenience (retail proximity, subscription models).                      |
| **Behavioral Preferences** | - 70% discover brands via TikTok/Instagram.                          |
|                     | - 60% prefer in-person activations over digital-only.                      |
|                     | - 80% engage with brands that align with social causes.                    |

#### **Secondary Segment: Millennial Nostalgia Seekers (30-40)**
- **Pain Points:** Desire to relive 90s/2000s pop culture.
- **Triggers:** Retro packaging, throwback campaigns, celebrity collabs.

### **11. METRICS & RISK MITIGATION PLAN**
**North Star Metric:** *Gen Z/Young Professional Market Share Growth (+20% in 90 days).*
**Secondary KPIs:**
- **CAC:** <$5 per new customer.
- **LTV:** $50+ (12-month horizon).
- **Conversion Rate:** 3% (digital), 5% (retail).

**Key Risks & Countermeasures:**
| **Risk**                          | **Countermeasure**                                                                 |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Low TikTok/Instagram Engagement** | A/B test meme formats, partner with micro-influencers (10K-100K followers).       |
| **Retail Partner Resistance**      | Offer co-branded displays, revenue-sharing incentives.                            |
| **Supply Chain Delays**            | Pre-negotiate bulk production slots, diversify regional suppliers.                |
