Founded by Razack Sattar in 1986, Prestige has developed residential colonies and commercial spaces in Bangalore, Chennai, Kochi, Calicut, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Mangalore, Goa and Delhi-NCR "National Capital Region (India)"). Notable projects include Prestige Shantiniketan, UB City, Prestige Golfshire, Prestige Acropolis, The Forum "Nexus Mall (Koramangala)"), The Forum Value, The Forum Vijaya, The Celebration Mall, Prestige Lakeside Habitat, The Prestige City and Prestige Kingfisher Towers.
