Executive Overview Gap Inc. is a brand portfolio company in global apparel retail, not just the single Gap brand. Founded in 1969 and headquartered in San Francisco, it operates four major banners: Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta. Across those brands, the company sells casual apparel, denim, activewear, accessories, and kids and baby products through company-operated stores, e-commerce sites, franchise locations, and a smaller set of wholesale relationships. What Is the History of Gap Inc.? Gap Inc. was founded in 1969 by Donald and Doris Fisher in San Francisco.
